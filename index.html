🔬 Cells

A cell is the basic unit of life. All living organisms are made of one or more cells.

Cell Types

  • Prokaryotic → does not have a nucleus; bacteria are examples.
  • Eukaryotic → has a nucleus; animal and plant cells are examples.

Important Organelles

PartFunction
NucleusContains DNA and controls many cell activities
Cell membraneControls what enters and leaves
MitochondriaProduce usable energy
RibosomesMake proteins
CytoplasmWhere many cell activities occur
VacuoleStores materials
Cell wallSupports plant cells
ChloroplastPerforms photosynthesis in plant cells

⭐ Remember: Nucleus = DNA, Mitochondria = Energy, Ribosomes = Proteins, Membrane = Gatekeeper.

🩺 BME Connection: Understanding cells helps engineers develop technologies involving tissues, diseases, medicines, and medical devices.

🧬 DNA & Genetics

DNA contains the genetic instructions used by living organisms.

Important Terms

  • Gene → a section of DNA
  • Chromosome → organized DNA and proteins
  • Trait → a characteristic
  • Heredity → passing genetic information from parents to offspring
  • Mutation → a change in DNA

⭐ Remember: DNA → Genes → Chromosomes → Genetic Information.

🩺 BME Connection: Genetics is important in biotechnology, genetic testing, disease research, and diagnostic technologies.

🫀 Human Body Systems

The human body has many systems that work together.

Examples

  • ❤️ Circulatory System → moves blood, oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and wastes.
  • 🫁 Respiratory System → brings oxygen in and removes carbon dioxide.
  • 🧠 Nervous System → receives information and coordinates responses.
  • 🍎 Digestive System → breaks food down and absorbs nutrients.
  • 🦴 Musculoskeletal System → supports the body and allows movement.
  • 🛡️ Immune System → helps protect the body from pathogens.
  • 🚽 Urinary System → removes wastes and regulates water and salts.

⭐ Remember: Body systems work together to keep the body functioning.

⚖️ Homeostasis

Homeostasis is maintaining relatively stable internal conditions.

Examples

  • 🌡️ Body temperature
  • 💧 Water balance
  • 🍬 Blood glucose
  • ❤️ Heart rate

Example: Body gets hot → sweating increases → heat is released → body cools.

🩺 BME Connection: Biomedical engineers can design sensors and monitoring devices that measure conditions in the body.

⭐ Remember: Homeostasis = internal balance.

🦴 Biomechanics

Biomechanics applies mechanics and physics to living organisms.

It studies force, motion, movement, bones, muscles, and joints.

Example: Muscle produces force → bone moves → joint allows movement.

🩺 BME Connection: Biomechanics is used to design prosthetic limbs, artificial joints, braces, and assistive devices.

⭐ Remember: Biomechanics = Biology + Mechanics.

⚙️ Engineering Design

Biomedical engineers use the engineering design process to solve problems.

  1. Identify the problem
  2. Research
  3. Brainstorm
  4. Design
  5. Build a prototype
  6. Test
  7. Improve

Example: Problem → someone needs help holding an object → engineer designs, builds, tests, and improves an assistive device.

⭐ Remember: Engineering is a cycle. Testing helps engineers improve designs.

🦾 Medical Devices

A medical device is technology designed for a healthcare purpose.

Examples

  • 🦿 Prosthetic limbs
  • ❤️ Pacemakers
  • 🦴 Artificial joints
  • 🦻 Hearing aids
  • ♿ Wheelchairs
  • 🩺 Monitoring devices
  • 🦾 Assistive devices

Engineers consider safety, function, comfort, reliability, and materials.

⭐ Remember: Medical device = technology used for a healthcare purpose.

🧪 Biomaterials

Biomaterials are materials used in medical applications that interact with biological systems.

Examples

  • Metals
  • Polymers
  • Ceramics
  • Hydrogels

Important Properties

A biomaterial may need to be strong, durable, flexible, safe, and suitable for its intended use.

Biocompatibility: how suitable a material is for its intended interaction with the body.

🩺 BME Connection: Biomaterials can be used in implants, prosthetics, and other medical technologies.

⭐ Remember: Biomaterial = material used for a biomedical purpose.

🩻 Medical Imaging

Medical imaging allows healthcare professionals to see structures inside the body.

Types

  • X-ray → uses X-rays to create images.
  • CT → uses X-rays + computer processing to create cross-sectional images.
  • MRI → uses a strong magnetic field + radio waves to create detailed images.
  • Ultrasound → uses high-frequency sound waves to create images.

⭐ Memorize: X-ray → X-rays; CT → X-rays + computer; MRI → magnets + radio waves; Ultrasound → sound waves.

🩺 BME Connection: Engineers help develop and improve imaging equipment and image-processing technologies.

🧫 Microbiology

Microbiology is the study of microscopic organisms and biological agents.

Examples

  • Bacteria → single-celled organisms; some helpful, some harmful.
  • Viruses → infectious agents that reproduce using host cells.
  • Fungi → organisms including yeasts and molds.
  • Pathogen → an infectious agent that can cause disease.

🩺 BME Connection: Biomedical engineers can develop diagnostic tests, biosensors, disease-detection technologies, and lab technologies.

⭐ Remember: Microbiology = study of microscopic life and biological agents.

🧠 Neuroscience

Neuroscience is the study of the nervous system.

Main Parts

Brain → Spinal cord → Nerves.

Neurons

A neuron is a specialized cell that communicates information through electrical and chemical signals.

The nervous system helps you sense your environment, process information, control movement, and respond to stimuli.

🩺 BME Connection: Biomedical engineers can develop neural sensors, neuroprosthetics, brain-computer interfaces, and assistive technologies.

⭐ Remember: Neuron = specialized cell that communicates information.

💻 Biomedical Technology & Coding

Computers are important tools in modern biomedical engineering.

Coding Can Be Used For:

  • 📊 Data analysis
  • 📈 Graphs
  • 🩻 Medical image processing
  • 🔬 Scientific simulations
  • 📡 Sensors
  • 🤖 AI systems
  • 🧮 Mathematical modeling

Python is a useful programming language for learning computational skills.


heart_rates = [72, 75, 78, 80, 76, 74]
average = sum(heart_rates) / len(heart_rates)
print("Average heart rate:", average)

🩺 BME Connection: Coding allows engineers to build and analyze technologies that work with biological and medical data.

⭐ Remember: Coding + Biology + Engineering = Biomedical Computing.
