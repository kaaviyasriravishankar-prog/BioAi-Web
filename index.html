🔬 Cells

A cell is the basic unit of life. All living organisms are made of one or more cells.

Cell Types

  • Prokaryotic: does not have a nucleus; bacteria are examples.
  • Eukaryotic: has a nucleus; animal and plant cells are examples.

Important Organelles

PartFunction
NucleusContains DNA and controls many cell activities.
Cell membraneControls what enters and leaves the cell.
CytoplasmJelly‑like fluid where organelles are found.
MitochondriaProduce energy for the cell.
RibosomesMake proteins.

🧬 DNA & Genetics

DNA carries genetic information that determines traits in living organisms.

🫀 Human Body Systems

The human body has multiple systems that work together to maintain life.

⚖️ Homeostasis

Homeostasis keeps internal conditions stable, like body temperature and blood sugar.

🦴 Biomechanics

Biomechanics applies physics and engineering to understand movement and forces in the body.

⚙️ Engineering Design

Engineering design is a cycle: identify → research → brainstorm → design → build → test → improve.

🦾 Medical Devices

Medical devices include prosthetics, pacemakers, monitors, and diagnostic tools.

🧪 Biomaterials

Biomaterials are materials that interact safely with the body, such as metals, polymers, ceramics, and hydrogels.

🩻 Medical Imaging

Medical imaging includes X‑ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound to visualize internal structures.

🧫 Microbiology

Microbiology studies bacteria, viruses, fungi, and other microscopic organisms.

🧠 Neuroscience

Neuroscience focuses on the brain, spinal cord, and nerves, and how they control the body.

💻 Biomedical Technology & Coding

Coding helps analyze biomedical data, control devices, and build simulations and models.
