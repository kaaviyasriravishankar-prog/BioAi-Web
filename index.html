UNIT 1 — BME FOUNDATIONS

01 • What Is Biomedical Engineering?

Biomedical engineering uses engineering principles to solve problems involving biology and medicine.

What does a biomedical engineer do?

  • Design a medical device
  • Develop a prosthetic limb
  • Improve medical imaging
  • Create sensors
  • Study biomaterials
  • Develop computer models
  • Help design rehabilitation technology

Key Idea

Biomedical engineering = using engineering to solve problems related to living systems and healthcare.

Connect the Subjects

  • Biology → Understand the body
  • Engineering → Design a solution
  • Technology → Build and analyze it
  • Medicine → Apply it to healthcare

UNIT 2 — BIOLOGY

02 • Cells

What is a cell? A cell is the basic unit of life.

Important cell structures

StructureJob
NucleusContains DNA
Cell membraneControls what enters and leaves
MitochondriaHelp provide usable energy
CytoplasmWhere many cell activities occur
RibosomesMake proteins

BME CONNECTION — Biomedical engineers study cells when working on tissue engineering, biomaterials, and diagnostics.

03 • DNA & Genetics

DNA

DNA contains genetic instructions used by cells.

Important vocabulary

  • Gene → section of DNA
  • Chromosome → structure containing DNA
  • Genetics → study of heredity and variation

BME CONNECTION — Biomedical engineering can involve technologies for studying DNA, genetic variation, and genetic testing.

UNIT 3 — THE HUMAN BODY

04 • Body Systems

The body is organized: Cells → Tissues → Organs → Organ Systems → Organism

Systems you'll study:

  • Circulatory
  • Respiratory
  • Nervous
  • Skeletal
  • Muscular
  • Immune
  • Digestive

ENGINEERING CHALLENGE

Problem: A body system isn't functioning normally.

Question: How could technology help monitor, support, or replace part of its function?

05 • The Cardiovascular System

Main parts: Heart, Blood, Blood vessels

Main job: Transport substances around the body.

Blood pathway: Body → Right Heart → Lungs → Left Heart → Body

BME CONNECTION — Biomedical engineers work on technologies such as heart-monitoring systems, sensors, medical devices, and computer-based analysis.

06 • The Nervous System

Main parts: Brain + Spinal Cord + Nerves

The nervous system allows the body to receive information, process it, and respond.

BME CONNECTION — Biomedical engineering can involve neural interfaces, brain-related technology, assistive devices, and prosthetic control.

UNIT 4 — ENGINEERING THE BODY

07 • Bones, Joints & Movement

Bones support the body, protect organs, help produce movement.

Engineering connection — Engineers can design prosthetics, artificial joints, braces, rehabilitation devices.

08 • Prosthetics

A prosthesis is an artificial replacement for a missing body part. A prosthetic system can involve mechanical parts, materials, sensors, and electronics.

Engineering question: How can we design a device that helps a person perform a movement?

UNIT 5 — SEEING INSIDE THE BODY

09 • Medical Imaging

Medical imaging creates images of structures inside the body. Major technologies include:

  • X-ray — Uses X-rays to create images.
  • CT — Combines many X-ray measurements to create detailed images.
  • MRI — Uses magnetic fields and radio waves.
  • Ultrasound — Uses high-frequency sound waves.

10 • Sensors & Wearable Technology

A sensor detects or measures something.

Examples: Smartwatch sensors, Glucose monitors, Heart-rate sensors, Oxygen-level sensors

Basic system: Body → Sensor → Signal → Computer → Information

Engineering question: How can we measure something happening inside or on the body without causing harm?

UNIT 6 — MATERIALS & MEDICINE

11 • Biomaterials

A biomaterial is a material designed to interact with a biological system for a medical purpose.

Engineers consider strength, durability, function, safety, biocompatibility. Applications: Implants, Dental devices, Artificial joints, Medical devices.

12 • Drug Delivery

The problem: Medicine needs to reach the right place and work effectively.

BME solutions: Specialized delivery systems, Controlled-release systems, Nanoparticle-based approaches.

13 • Tissue Engineering

Tissue engineering uses engineering and biological principles to develop or support tissues. Basic concept: Cells + Materials + Biological signals → Tissue engineering

UNIT 7 — ROBOTS & TECHNOLOGY

14 • Surgical Robots

Surgical robots assist with procedures. Engineers work on mechanical systems, sensors, cameras, control systems, and software.

15 • Computers in Biomedical Engineering

Computers help analyze data, process images, create models, control devices, and study biological information. Programming languages like Python are useful.

16 • Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

AI systems can analyze patterns in large datasets. Applications include medical-image analysis, data analysis, prediction, and research.

⚠️ Important — AI is a tool. Medical technologies must be carefully tested and evaluated for safety and reliability.

UNIT 10 — BME ETHICS

19 • Safety & Ethics

Biomedical engineers work with technology that can affect people's health. They must think about privacy, safety, fairness, accuracy, reliability, and accessibility.

Golden Rule: A biomedical invention must be designed with safety and responsible testing in mind.
