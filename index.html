Unit 2 — Biology

Cells

The cell is the basic unit of life. Important structures: nucleus (DNA), membrane, mitochondria, cytoplasm, ribosomes.

DNA & Genetics

DNA contains genetic instructions. Gene = section of DNA; Chromosome = structure containing DNA.

Unit 3 — The Human Body

Organization: Cells → Tissues → Organs → Systems (circulatory, respiratory, nervous, skeletal, muscular, immune, digestive).

Cardiovascular system

Heart, blood, vessels — engineers make monitoring systems and sensors.

Unit 4 — Engineering the Body

Prosthetics, implants and rehabilitation devices combine materials, mechanics, sensors and software.

Unit 5 — Seeing Inside the Body

Medical imaging: X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound — image processing is a core BME area.

Sensors & Wearables

Wearables measure signals: heart-rate, oxygen, glucose and more.

Unit 6 — Materials & Medicine

Biomaterials for implants, controlled drug delivery, nanoparticles and tissue engineering are key topics.

Unit 7 — Robots & Technology
