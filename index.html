Biomedical Engineering Notes

Explore each topic below

🔬 Cells

A cell is the basic unit of life. All living organisms are made of one or more cells.

  • Prokaryotic → no nucleus
  • Eukaryotic → has a nucleus

BME Connection: Understanding cells helps engineers develop technologies involving tissues and diseases.

🧬 DNA & Genetics

DNA contains genetic instructions used by living organisms.

  • Gene → section of DNA
  • Chromosome → organized DNA
  • Trait → characteristic

BME Connection: Genetics is key in biotechnology and diagnostics.
