02 / Biology Notes

Learn the
important ideas.

01

Cell Theory

All living things are made of one or more cells. The cell is the basic unit of life, and new cells come from existing cells.

02

Homeostasis

Homeostasis is the maintenance of stable internal conditions. For example, your body regulates its temperature even when the environment changes.

03

Photosynthesis

Plants use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen.

04

Food Webs

A food web shows how organisms in an ecosystem are connected through feeding relationships.
